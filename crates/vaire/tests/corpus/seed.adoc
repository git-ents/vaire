[requirement#SWR-0142]
[modality=shall, kind=functional, status=approved]
[source=STK-014, verification=test, volatility=low]
[refines=SYR-0031, allocated-to=arm.control]
--
The joint controller shall publish a `JointState` sample within 2 ms of
each encoder read.

.Rationale
[.rationale]
Impedance control diverges above 2 ms of feedback age.

.Acceptance
[.acceptance]
* p99 publish latency <= 2 ms over 10^6 samples at 1 kHz
* zero dropped samples at 3x nominal CPU load
--
